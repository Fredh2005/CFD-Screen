      How the score and the levels are worked out

      Everything is calculated from daily bars. The rules favour trading with the trend: the screen looks for either a pullback inside an existing trend or a break of the 20-day range, and never for a reversal. Each name is scored long and short, out of 100:

      score = (trend×0.30 + setup×0.30 + relative strength×0.15 + market×0.25) × 10 − stretch×3 − event×2.5

      Trend (0–10): whether the price is on the right side of its 50-day average, whether the 50-day is rising (long) or falling (short), whether the 20-day is above or below the 50-day, and the price against the 200-day. Setup (0–10): a pullback scores when the trend is at least 7 and the price is near its 20-day average with RSI(14) pulled back towards 42 (58 for a short). A breakout scores 8 when the price clears the prior 20-day high (or low for a short) with the trend at 5 or more, falling to 5 if RSI is already past 75 (25 for a short). Relative strength: a share's 3-month return against its home index; an index's own 3-month return. Market: the trend score of the share's home index, or the average of all four for an index.

      Stretch is a penalty for being more than 1.5 ATR past the 20-day average in the direction of the trade, which is chasing. Event is a penalty when an earnings date falls inside your hold (10) or within a week after it (3), or when a central bank decision for that market falls inside your hold (5 for an index, 2 for a share).

      The levels are mechanical, not forecasts. If it works is the price plus your target multiple of ATR(14), the average daily range over 14 days. If it fails is the price minus your stop multiple. Change the multiples in Your account, or override them on any card. Position size is set so that a fill at the stop loses your risk per trade, converted to pounds at the build's exchange rate. Gaps can fill you well past the stop.

      Net R:R is the £ gained at the target minus spread and funding, divided by the £ lost at the stop plus spread and funding.
